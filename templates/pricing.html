What counts as a monthly active user?+

Anyone who signs in, signs up, or refreshes a valid session during the calendar month. Machine-to-machine tokens and webhook calls are never counted.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. Upgrades apply instantly with prorated billing, and downgrades take effect at the end of your current cycle. Your users will never be logged out mid-change.

Do you offer self-hosted deployment?+

The Scale plan supports dedicated single-tenant clusters and on-prem connectors. Talk to sales for VPC, region pinning, and bring-your-own-key options.
